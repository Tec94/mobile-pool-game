Questions

Where is my progress?
On your iPhone only. There is no account or cloud save, so deleting the app deletes your cues, stars and level.
Is there anything to buy inside the game?
No. You pay once. Every cue is earned by playing, with coins or achievements.
How do I aim and shoot?
Drag on the table to aim, turn the dial for fine aim, then pull the power rail back and let go. The ball button sets spin and cue angle. Learn on the home screen teaches each shot.
Can I play in portrait?
Yes. Settings, then Orientation.
Does it need the internet?
No. Everything works offline.
