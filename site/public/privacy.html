Privacy

Updated 23 November 2026

The game

Runout: All about Pool collects no data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your settings, cues and progress are saved only on your iPhone. Delete the app to delete them. The App Clip works the same way, and iOS removes it and its data after a while if you do not use it.

If you send a crash report or rate the app, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy and shares only anonymous reports with us.

This website

If you join the waitlist, we keep the email you enter, your first name if you give one, the country Cloudflare reads from your connection, the link you came from (for example ?ref=ig) and the time. We use it only to email you when Runout is out. We do not sell or share it.

The site runs on Cloudflare. To stop abuse, it counts sign-ups per IP address for one minute, and Cloudflare keeps request logs (which include IP addresses) for a few days. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics or ads.

To be removed from the list, or to see what we hold about you, email support@runout.win from the address you signed up with and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, children included. The waitlist is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this changes, for example when online play arrives, we will update this page and the App Store privacy label before the new version ships.
